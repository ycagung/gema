# Arc 3 - Chapter 2 - New Scar and an Old One

---

**Gema**
---
"I'm so going to jail," I said, facepalmed. 

"What are you talking about?" She asks, sitting beside me, clinging on to my arm and resting her head against my shoulder.

"I just woke up from a two year coma and the first thing I did was kissing a minor."

"I'm twenty two, you moron. You were out for two years, or did that fact hasn't settled in your thick skull yet?" Her tone almost hostile. I can relate to it though, she would've been terrified that when I woke up, the first thing I'd do was to push her out. 

"Still. You know... It's weird for me."

She steps down from the bed, turns around to face me and holds my face firmly. "Listen here, Gema Cahaya. I don't care what you're thinking right now. But we both know that you are just waking up from long, deep sleep. So we don't need to rush anything. Just be yourself, get your bearing, I'm here for you. Always been, always will be. I earned that right, don't you dare take it away from me."

Alright, she really managed to slap me again with her words. "Yes, ma'am."

"Good boy. Now if you'll excuse me, I have some important guest to pick up in the lobby," she says after glancing at her phone. And without even waiting for me to answer, she left me. Since when did she become so... insisting?

Fine, I guess. Now I have some time to think. Now where were we? I was out for two years, meaning it's 2016. I glance at the monitoring device and confirm that today is March 16<sup>th</sup>. I can work with that. I'll visit Jukebox tomorrow to see how everyone's doing. If they're on track, Roundtable should be in its third phase right now. Due to release in two months. 

Maybe my parents will come in two hours, I don't wanna think about how to explain all of these to my mother. Oh, sh#t! Hospital bills! No! Do I really have to drown in debt again? But then, it's better than to just die for nothing at all.

Then I hear footsteps from the corridor, along with Tika's voice, chatting with... My mom?

"No, Tika, dear. You don't have to always come down and pick me in the lobby."

What? Tika *dear*? Is that really my mom I'm hearing?

"I want to, *bu*. Well, It's more of a habit for me anyway. Wait! Wait..."

She sounds like she stopped my mother from opening the door. Wait, did I hear that right? She's calling my mother *ibu*? What in hell's name is going on?

"I... should've tell you this earlier, but I couldn't bring myself to type in the message."

"What is it, dear? Is it a bad news? Did something happen to my boy?"

"No! No.. *Ibu*. He's... he's awake."

Then the door slams open, again, just like when Tika entered earlier. But now it's just my mother, standing in the doorway, speechless. Her gaze shifting from looking like she saw a dead man into looking like she finally got the hope she was waiting desperately. Before tears can run down her face, she runs to me and embrace me in her arms.

"It's good to see you, *bu*."

"My boy... I missed you. My boy... Thank God. I don't know what'll become of me if I lose you."

Now I get it. I didn't understand why Tika acted like that earlier. Now I really get it. Looking at my mother, sobbing her tears out on my chest like I was a long lost son that just came home. I look up and see Tika standing in the doorway, tears run down her face again. But now she smiles, warmly. Slowly she backs off and closes the door. Leaving me and my mother.

---

"Have you seen it?" Asks my mother.

"See what, mom?"

"Your scar."

Right. I haven't. I didn't even think about it earlier. Slowly I unbuttoned my hospital shirt and try to feel my left chest. It's there, the scar. Something very unfamiliar to me. I didn't have it in my previous life, I didn't have it in this life. Until now. 

"Why, my son?"

"What do you mean, mom?"

"Why, with all this, with everything you've done for her sake, even took bullets for her, you still haven't accepted her feelings? Don't you see that it's torturing her?"

"Mom, what are you-"

"I've been coming here three times every week for the last two years. In the first months when you went into coma, I couldn't bring myself to leave you. And do you know who convinced me to leave? To not drown in the sorrow? To believe that we can have hope while living life?"

I can't say anything. I feel like I know the answer, but I can't say it.

"It was her. She was drowning in the same sorrow as me, then pulled me out of it because she managed to do it herself. I could recognize it in her eyes. Do you know what it takes for a mother to acknowledge a love for her son? She was here almost everyday, Gema. Everyday. On top of mobilizing every facility in this absurdly big hospital to treat you. Without giving us even a cent of bill. For what? She told me that you couldn't even gave her a straight answer. I couldn't even bring myself to tell her to move on. She didn't deserve this, my son. I didn't raise you like that."

Now I really don't know what to say. Never in my imagination that the first thing my mother do when I woke up from a two year coma is to scold me. And I hate that she's right. She always is. 

"You're right, *bu*. I was wrong. I'll make things right."

"I know you will."

---

"*Bu*, look at these. Finally managed to get them."

Tika comes into the room bearing a big plastic bag. 

"Is that what I think it is?" Asks my mother, looking like a kid getting her favorite candy. Tika nods. They both open the bag and take out a bunch of food packaging. I recognize those packages, it's from a famous local restaurant that's always sold out at this time of day. It's *gado-gado*, basically Indonesian salad. The thing is, my mother is so fond of that particular restaurant but always didn't get to buy them because she's working. 

"Thank you, dear. You really are the daughter I've never had."

They both laugh. The laugh itself is weird. I realize that they must've been together the last two years. But this looks like... I don't know what this looks like. Both of them look like a real mother and daughter.

How much did I miss?

---

It's late, 9 PM. I've had a bunch of visitors over the last few hours. My father and little brother came. Teasing about something something conglomerate girlfriend something. Which made me punch them in the arms. Because Tika was there in front of us, and she didn't react at all. Like it was okay!

Luna, Hans, and Otis came together too. Luna brought me a big bucket of flower, which I said thanks to but I couldn't eat it. She laughed, and punched me in the stomach. What's with all the punching and slapping? These people, jeez. Hans brought me a pack of our favorite sandwich across the office. Otis... Well he brought me a bug. Three bugs to be precise. Which I told him how to solve right away.

Even Tika's father came too. My biggest benefactor. Mr. Kusumo. He didn't brought me anything. Yeah, well, he didn't need to. He asked how I was feeling. I said I'm good. Then he said thank you before leaving, which made me confused. Tika then said it was because I took a bullet for his daughter. Ah, that made sense.

Now all of them are gone. I can relax. Then, click. The door opens again. Tika's there, now wearing comfortable clothes. The kind of comfortable clothes a girl wears in her HOUSE.

"Hey, hey! What are you doing? Aren't you supposed to go home? And why are you wearing that. I told you that's not appropriate." Yeah I scolded her before. Because we're going out and she was wearing tank top. 

"I'm going to sleep here, this is my sleeping clothes." She says while gesturing at her body, wearing a tank top and a pair of very short pants. "Why? I've slept here often. Well, you were out cold so you don't know."

"And you want to sleep here *tonight*? What's wrong with you? Your dad will kill me."

"Well, if he wants to. You'd already be dead. I changed your clothes, y'know. I even bathe you when your mother couldn't make it here."

"Whaaaat? Why?"

"Because a half-dead man still needs to be bathed, love," she says, closing on me by inches. 

"Yeah, I know that, but-"

"Why? Do you feel embarrassed?" Now she's holding my chin. I'm shuddering from imagining how she was caressing my body while I was out. That sounds creepy.

"Yes, I do feel embarrassed. Didn't you?" I gently move her hand from my chin. 

"I did, for the few first times." Finally she releases he teasing tension and sits beside me. "And for the record, I didn't clean your... Uh... Thing. So don't worry, love. I'm not that forceful. Y'know..."

"What?"

"For a person as smart and cunning as you, even with all the future sight you have. Why did you pick me? Why did you even do all those things to protect me? You could've pushed away when we first talked all those years ago. It's been weighing on my mind, and I have a lot of time to think when you were in a coma. What am I, Gema?"

"What do you mean?"

"What am I in your past life? Tell me the truth."

I once again don't know what to say. 

"Everything that I had in my past life, I broke them all. Everything that I touched, broken. Anya wasn't like that. She was able to pull herself out, seven years after it happened. I was the one she blamed. My parents. About a year ago. They would've been drowning in debt because of me. Because of my stupidity and blind ambitions. And that's not even talking about my wife. My ex-wife."

"You already told me that you have one. What about her?"

"I had a son, Tika..."

"What? You've never told me that."

I choke on my throat trying to continue. Tears that I can't control filling up my eyes. That memory is still too painful for me to remember. For anyone to remember.

"Hey, hey... What's wrong?" Tika realizes that I am breaking down. She quickly moves in front of me and holds me in her chest. "Hey, it's okay. I'm sorry. I didn't want to hurt you."

"It's okay, you deserve to know." I said after pulling my face from her bosom. "My wife. She was sick. Not physically, mentally. I turned out she had multiple undiagnosed mental issues from years of abuse by her father."

"That means you didn't break her, right?"

"How do you think I know of her condition? I triggered it. Badly."
