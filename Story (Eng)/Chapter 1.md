# Chapter 1 - One Plus One Equals Zero

---

**Gema**
---
It is common for a sky as grey as this to be an omen of a heavy rain, and looks like this one is not an exception. Like confirming that I'm not wrong, a flash of light from faraway came blinding my eyes for a split second followed by the bang and vibration three seconds later. It's fine, raining or not, I'm not planning to go home early today. 

I spin my chair to face my laptop again, ignoring the unique earthy smell signaling the upcoming rain that creeps to my nose. Suddenly my phone rings. A client's name appears on it. Tapping the answer and loudspeaker button, I put down my phone on the desk again.

"Evening, Leo. How can I help you?" I said, starting the conversation.

*"Evening, Gem,"* said he flatly. *"Can you explain to me why is my financial report still using the wrong format?! Did my instruction to use the format my team gave you not clear enough? Do you really..."*

I laid down my head onto the headrest, or 'succumbed' would be the better word. It's just this kind of nonsensical nagging and complaining one after another. Does he not have a better things to do? Yes his team gave me their 'special' format about a week ago. And yes I received it. Though it's very unethical and just bad practice for me to implement it. Why? Because basically I would be facilitating a fraud. His 'trusted' team purposely created that format to cover up their gray expenses. What nonsense is a two thousand dollar dinner for a thousand-dollar-per-month-revenue client? They really expect me to allow that in my system? The one I built with my hands? But alas, Leo wouldn't listen to me at all. He was even threatened to cut the contract because he sees me as an outsider continuously discrediting his team.

"I'll look into it again. Just give me a few days," I said finally, just to close the conversation. Followed by a deep long breath full of frustration. My temples feel like they are about to explode. 

With a very heavy heart, I click the folder "Project Leo - Financial Platform". Combing those neat and organized code line by line until the reporting section come up. I read that specific section from top to bottom, then bottom to top. The sequences are perfect, the calculations are precise. Why should I change this at all? 

Then my phone ring again. This time from Andrea. Great. Just great.

"Hey, boss," I said, as light as I can.

*"Gem! Did you just lost your mind?! Or you don't have it in the first place?! My ears are burning from hearing Leo talking shit for the whole five goddamn minutes! For what?! Just because you won't use the format his team sent last week?! What kind of nonsense is this, Gem?!"*

"Calm down, boss-"

*"Shut your mouth! You call me boss but you never listen to me! Not once! I told you Leo is a major client! The only way we'll play is by their rules!"*

"Andrea, listen to me. I sent them the migration file from their old system. Did you read it? You didn't, right? If you read it, you'll know that Leo's team is embezzling absurd amount of money into their own pockets. Their financial data is unbalanced, very much so. That's why they asked us to follow their made-up financial report format. To cover it up."

*"Gema, I don't know what makes you think that I care about their internal issue at all. What I care about is how much Leo is paying us. And if you don't fix that report by tomorrow, the amount will be ZERO!"*

Like I said, nonsensical nagging and complaints. He doesn't even hear a word that came out of my mouth. Let alone understand that I really don't want to facilitate this kind of fraud to my client, OUR client. A fraud that was done and still being done by his employees no less. 

Finally, I choose to stand down. Like usual.

This isn't the first time, and hardly will be the last time. As hard as it would be to understand what kind of short-sighted, deeply money-oriented way Andrea is thinking, it is harder for me to take action against it. What more can I do?

This is the kind of life I have at the office. A near eternal cycle of facing barrages of complaints from the clients, then another barrage of complaints from Andrea, whose ego is drenched in shame from the same complaints from the same clients that just directed at me. All those in a matter of hours, four times a day, seven times a week, thirty- Ah, you get the idea.

---

The clock said it's 9 PM. I think I have enough fill of today's nonsense. In this place at least. I clean up my desk and get ready to leave. It's time for me to be at home, facing another session of endless complaining from my wife. Be it a stack of unpaid bills, her unfulfilled 'dreams', neighbors' gossip, or just about ten thousand other things she pins on me like I'm the source of evil in this world. She would go on and on until she gets tired of it and leaves me to sleep. And then I will get my usual loneliness, sitting by myself on the porch smoking two cigarettes that I bought on the way home. This is the only time I can truly call mine, at least I still have it.

After all that, you would think that I would blame my wife, Andrea, Leo, or whoever it is. Well, I'm not, at least not for my miserable life. I always think that all of these must be my responsibilities, my cross to bear, my karma, whatever you want to call it. 

I could have a better career had I have a more 'flexible' mindset. I could have a happier family had I not wasted the love my wife gave me. The wife that I married too young back then. Followed by another series of should'a, would'a, could'a. 

If this life is a game, I would self-kill to restart from level 1. Or I would rather just die now.

---

The metro station is just a kilometer away. My socks are already wet from walking on the wet after-rain street. I can feel the cold in the air penetrating down my skin. At least I can enjoy it, this is nothing compared to the cold in my wife's voice later. I can actually enjoy this cold air after all.

Then my mind goes away to that reset thought. If I really can restart my life again, what would I do different? Where should I start? What would be my priority? Would it be for my own benefit or atone for all the bad decision I've made before?

That last question lingers for longer that I want. It made my mind blank. *What would I really choose?*

My wet feet keep on walking to wherever the pavement is. Or where the pavement was, it's no longer there. I'm now standing on a road, a really big road. 

Then, a blinding light comes from my right, a deafening horn honking from the same direction, a lot of yelling from my back, all three happen in a split second. The next thing I know is that I can see my own neck, drenching in my own blood.

I've read that a decapitated head can still live for about five to ten seconds. Looks like I can attest to that as a fact. What more is that I thought it will be the longest five-to-ten seconds I've ever experienced because of excruciating pain. But it's not. I can't feel anything, let alone pain. It's just... nothing. Just the coldness of the asphalt and the wetness from the pool that is my blood. 

*Ah, what a real shitty ending of my already shitty life. So fitting. If my neck is still attached, I would laugh. But at least I already have the answer to that question. I would...*
