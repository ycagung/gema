# Arc 3 Chapter 3 - 

---

**Kartika**
---
"What do you mean you triggered that? Can you even trigger something like that?"

"Do you really want to hear the full story? Do you really believe that you can handle it?"

"Was she prettier than me?"

He chuckles, sounds like he's genuinely amused by my question. "What kind of question is that? Of course not. Do you think I married a supermodel or what?"

Aw, that's so sweet, I'm melting right now.

"That's a rhetoric question, love. I've seen what you are capable of. And you are capable of something truly evil. Yet, I can't see any evil within you. Everything you did up until now was to right your wrong. As self-imposing as those were. So I don't even need to believe myself, I believe in you."

"That's very sweet of you to say."

"Really. That was also very sweet of you to compare me to a supermodel. Makes me want to kiss you again right now."

"Please don't. I'm living in a very healthy body of a young man. Even I can't control how I will react past certain... threshold."

"You really think so?" Look at him. He's so cute, I want to tease him again. "Want me to test that?"

Then he cups his hands on my cheeks. *Aw, here it is!*

"Tika, I understand. I really do. I promise I won't push you out again. But please give me some time, I have to settle something first. Then I'll tell you anything you want to know. And..."

*And what, Gema? You really gonna leave me hanging like that.*

"I'll be ready for you."

---

**Gema**
---
Sunlight suddenly blares into my eyes, forcing them to open. 

"Morning sunshine," Tika says, her hands opening the blinds. She really wants to blind my eyes.

"Can we do a bit more subtle morning next time?" I speak lazily. Scratching my eyes.

"Really? So, when is next time?" She asks, after jumping onto my bed, excitedly. Urgh, this girl and her audacity. 

"What do you mean when?"

"You're being discharged today, love."

Thank the heavens. I can move now. "Finally."

"I thought you'll be moving in with me."

"Don't push it."

---

I don't know if the world outside this hospital with big "Adijaya" plastered on the wall is familiar or not. I don't feel like missing years of my life, but apparently I am. Honestly, it's easier to accept that I was regressed back to my 16-year-old-self six years ago. 

"I hope somebody took care of my car," I mumble. My beloved Miata, unused for two years. I can feel maintenance cost wants to pour out of my already thinning pocket. God, why is 2017 taking so long to arrive?

Then like the heaven suddenly hears my call. My black Miata rolls down the hospital's drop-off driveway. With its roof open. Driven by...

"Come on, I though you couldn't wait to go to the street," says Tika, pulling down her sunglasses a bit.

I jump in the passenger seat. "Were you dailying my baby these last two years?"

"Your welcome, love. Besides..." The car goes on to the street, steadily.

"Besides?"

"Driving this car is the only way I could keep my head sane. It feels like you were beside me all the time."

"Well, I am now."

---

The car pulls into a basement parking lot of a certain high-rise building in Surabaya. 

"I think I only saw the skeleton of this building last time I was here."

"Time moves fast. Faster if you have more money."

"I'm sure you do. Who decided to move here?"

"We didn't move, Gem. We expand. The old office is still there, housing... Well, Otis. He likes the chair."

"Expand? I don't have that in my roadmap. Expanding what exactly?"

"You'll see."

She parks the car in a particular spot labelled "Roundtable - CTO". 

"I didn't remember we have a CTO."

"Well, you are now."

"What? Why?"

She turns of the car and faces me.

"Because, my love. You don't know how big Roundtable is now. We need an official board, official building, everything that are necessary to run this operation. Come on, just follow me and ask after you're there."

"Fine."

We go to the elevator and Tika presses button number "24". For a short while
