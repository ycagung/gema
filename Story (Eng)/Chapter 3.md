# Chapter 3 - Five Minutes

---

**Gema**
---
Bell is ringing, school's finished. While everyone is cheering, closing their book and preparing to go home, I'm staying still. Eyes forward just because my mind is elsewhere.

"Come on, Gem," Jonas says as he stand up.

"You go. I have to meet Mr. Andrew."

"IT teacher? Whet's wrong? He called you? Did you mess something, Gem?" He's hitting me with a barrage of question as his face inching closer to mine. Which meet my palm as I push him away.

"I got a business called 'none of your business'. Go away," I said, waving my hand like swatting flies.

"Suit yourself, lonely man," then he left me.

Not two minutes later, the classroom is already empty. I stop running numbers in my head, take my bag and stand up. With confident steps, I start to walk to the computer lab, where Mr. Andrew usually sits. Then a familiar voice calls from behind me.

"Gema, not going home yet?" That voice immediately stops my legs. Anya.

"Not yet," I turn to face her. "I got something to do in computer lab. What about you? Still doing council... things?"

I'm good with numbers, terrific. Yet when talking about human senses, the best one I got is hearing. I can take a lot of data just by hearing something. Music, devices, vehicles, you name it. Including people. I have already know that Anya just done something physically heavy by noticing her breath pattern even before I turned my body. I also know that there are two more student walking up the stairs behind Anya by the sound of their footsteps. 

So what the exposition above is about? I have a bad eyesight.
