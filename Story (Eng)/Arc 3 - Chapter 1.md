# Arc 3 - Chapter 1 - Acceptance

---

**Gema**
---
Consciousness comes before light. In other words, I'm surrounded by a deep unsettling darkness. It feels weird, like I'm drowning minus the water pressure pressing me. I can feel my whole body fortunately, though I realized that by the pain all over it. Slowly I forced my eyes to open to no avail. I forced my hand to move. Failed too. Fine.

Then my hearing comes back. I can hear a beeping sound beside me. Beep. Beep. Beep. What's that? The rhythmic, it's so stable. Like... like my heartbeat. Weird. Am I in a hospital?

A little light comes through my eyes, finally I can open it. Though it feels like a thousand needles piercing through them, I forced them to open fully. I don't care. I need to know what's happening and where I am.

Slow but surely the blurry images sharpens. White ceiling, white lights, white walls. All white everywhere I see. Then I can see the source of that beeping sound. Of course it's a monitoring machine beside my bed. I really am in a hospital. That's a fact.

But why?

I still can't move my body, so here I am. Floating mind in my loneliness for what feels like forever before that white door suddenly opens. Good, a nurse that looks surprised to see me. Is it that bad? What even am I? A multi-year comma patient that has just woken up? As quickly as she came, she goes away leaving the door open.

---

"So, tell me how you feel, Gema," says a doctor right after she checked the monitoring device beside my bed and mumbling that all seems fine. 

"I feel I don't know what's going on. Why am I in a hospital anyway? Beside that? I feel fine."

"Alright," she says while jotting something on her board. "You sound lively, that's a good thing at least. Can you remember anything particular at all?"

"No, I don't. That's why I'm asking you what's going on here."

She stops writing and starts to look at me seriously, like she's finally realizing that I'm not lying. Then, she sits down.

"Gema, this will be a lot to take in then. You... were the victim of a shooting. You got shot in two places. One in your thigh, one in your chest. Your shooter was... Let's just say you were very lucky that the bullet was just grazing your heart,"

Her explanation sends me one wave of memory after another. It's like my life is flashing right before my eyes. Yes, I can remember. The bridge. Two loud bangs from a handgun held by someone I know. Someone who was clearly never touched that kind of weapon before. She's right, I was lucky. Just a milimeter off then this second chance of life would have been wasted for nothing. Then I remember falling from that bridge before all went black.

There was someone else though. I was protecting someone, purposely getting into the direct line of shot. 

"I was... I was with someone, doc. What happened to her?"

She jotted one more thing to her board before continuing. "It's good to know that you don't have amnesia. Yes, you were with someone. She's okay. She was brought here with you after jumping into the river trying to save you."

A breath of relief escapes my mouth. Tika is okay. Though I'll scold her thoroughly later for her recklessness. Just what was she thinking? Jumping into the river? I'm the one who's supposed to protect her, not the other way.

"She was released just after two days of treatment. Although for the first three weeks, she absolutely refused to leave your side. You're very lucky again, Gema. You're the whole world for her."

"Wait, what?! Three weeks?"

"Yes. She finally left your side because her father asked to. Saying that you wouldn't want her to just sit and do nothing. She visited often though. Almost everyday for the last two years."

Those last two words hit me like a sledgehammer. Two years?! I really have been in a comma for TWO F#CKING YEARS?! No words can escape my mouth. First I was shot, then I went into comma for that long. 

The door slams open, completely surprising me and the doctor. Then a girl storms into the room. A girl I know very well. A girl that is like the world to me, as I am to her. Though we can't actually reach each other like a normal people would. A girl that despite all the riches she has, all the places she can be, she chose to be by my side. I was once loved a woman so deep, I was traumatized by the fact that she left me for a useless, spineless jerk and made my heart numb. This girl managed to cure that very numbness just by her sheer will. She managed to convince me to share the burden I'm carrying. Making her the only person who knows and believes of my regression. I promised her that even against all odds, I'll protect her with all my might. At least I know I did that for the cost of two whole years of my life, I don't have any regret.

She runs straight to me with tears running down her eyes, smearing the make up on her beautiful face. She slams me with all her body weight. Wrapping her hands around my body like she's trying to crush me. 

"Hey, calm do-"

"Shut up! Just shut up!" She says, clearly forcing herself to form words in the middle of her sobbing tears. 

The doctor tries to calm her down by stroking her back. "Calm down, miss. Gema is okay. You did a good job keeping him company all this time."

For the next ten minutes, we all don't speak another words. Just listening to Tika crying her heart out.

When she's finally calmed down, she pulls herself from me and looks at the doctor.

"Thank you, Jane. Can you give us a moment?"

The doctor nods and leaves us to ourselves.

Tika looks straight at me. Her expression is a mix of anger and relief, I can't decide which, looks like she couldn't too.Then, SLAP! She slaps my left cheek hard. 

"What was that for?!" I said, flabbergasted. 

"If you put me through this again. If you purposely do something stupid like that again. I'll kill you myself."

I go quiet for a moment, looking at her straight in the eye. I can see the burden she carried for the last two years. I can see all the worry, fear, hopelessness she experienced. That was why she visited my bed almost every day. I think because that was the only thing keeping her sane. If I'm in her position, I would too.

"You can't ask me that. I promised you. I promised to protect you. That's the least I can do for you, Tika."

"No. That's no longer true."

"What? What are you-"

She places her hands on my cheeks, pulls herself towards me. Before I can pull myself back, she has her lips onto mine. She kisses me. She kisses me?! Her soft lips land against mine. Her warm breath brushing against my skin. I don't know what to do, what to feel. For the last three years before I went into comma, I've suppressed my feelings towards her. Because of common sense, because it's a crime for a middle aged man to romance a high schooler. Because I think that she'll find someone more... proper than me for her. But she kept going on without fear. Even after knowing my secrets, she didn't waver at all. There's really nothing that can stop Tika once she sets something as her goal. Finally, I gave up. Finally... I surrendered my feelings for her. I accept her kiss.
